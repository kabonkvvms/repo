Music Bridge

Music Bridge brings Spotify, Amazon Music, and Apple Music to jailbroken devices running iOS 6. Your Windows PC runs the web player and streams its audio over Wi-Fi. On your device, you can search for songs, browse playlists, see cover art, and control playback.

Full setup guide for Windows and iOS 6

Add the Cydia source

In Cydia, open Manage → Sources → Edit → Add and enter this page’s address.

Use the full address of this page, including the repo name.

Install Music Bridge. Open the app, then enter the local address printed by your Windows Music Bridge launcher under Settings.
